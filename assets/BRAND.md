# ppt-agent 品牌说明

橙色的幻灯片边框和内容横线代表正在编辑的演示，青色光标从边框右下角伸出，
表达 agent 直接参与操作。透明背景适合 README 的浅色和深色界面。

最终 Logo：[`logo.png`](logo.png)。由 imagegen 生成，未使用 Microsoft 官方图标。
流程横幅是本项目编写的 SVG 示意图，与实际 UI 截图区分。

Draw exactly ONE simple flat icon: a wide rounded slide rectangle in warm orange, containing two short horizontal content strokes aligned toward its left. The lower right frame has a carefully designed opening, from which one small bright cyan computer cursor arrow emerges diagonally. A single very small cyan four-point action sparkle may sit near the tip. All parts form one unified memorable mark.
Strong constraints: absolutely no letter P, no text, no people or human silhouettes, no monitor stand, no robot, no circles representing heads, no official Microsoft or PowerPoint icon, no overlapping badges, no illustrative extras. Do NOT create the familiar PowerPoint logo. Aim for 4-6 clean simple geometric shapes total.
Flat vector-like professional visual identity, uniform thick strokes, minimal geometry, crisp smooth edges, matte solid colors with absolutely no gradient, texture, 3D or shadows. Transparent background. Square 1024px composition with mark occupying roughly 72% and generous clear margin. It must remain readable at 32px on light and dark backgrounds. One final logo only, no brand sheet, mockup, or background rectangle.
